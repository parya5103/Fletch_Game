How to play

  • Tap an arrow — it flies off the board in the direction it points.
  • It only leaves if its path to the edge is clear. Another arrow in the way blocks it.
  • A blocked tap costs one heart (♥♥♥). Lose all three and the level restarts.
  • Clear every arrow to finish the level. Order is everything!
  • Stars: ★★★ = no mistakes & no hints · ★★ = up to 2 mistakes & 1 hint · ★ = cleared.
  • Hint glows a free arrow · Undo brings the last arrow back · Restart resets.
  • Your progress saves automatically — come back any time.

No timer, no pressure. Take your time and untangle the board.
