Arrow Out! — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · Plain-English summary: the game stores everything on your device and collects almost nothing.

What the game stores

Arrow Out! has no accounts, no sign-in and no server database. All your progress — current level, stars, coins, owned skins, streaks, achievements and settings — is saved in your browser's localStorage, on your own device. It never leaves your device.

  • The game makes no external network requests while you play (the site ships with a strict Content-Security-Policy).
  • There are no analytics, no trackers, no cookies and no advertising IDs today (see Advertising below).
  • Installing the game (PWA) copies it to your device for offline play; it does not add any new data collection.

Daily reminders (optional notifications)

Reminders are off unless you turn them on in Settings. If you enable them, your browser creates a Web Push subscription. That subscription record (a unique device endpoint, no personal details) is stored by the developer and used for exactly one purpose: delivering the 4–5 daily game reminders you asked for. It contains no name, email or gameplay data.

  • Turning reminders off in Settings removes the subscription and it is deleted from the send list.
  • You can also revoke notification permission for the site any time in your browser settings.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings → Reset all progress clears everything stored on your device.
  • In your browser: clearing site data for this site removes the save and any notification subscription.

Advertising

Arrow Out! shows no ads today. The game is prepared to add optional rewarded ads (for example, watch a short ad to earn one extra hint) through a provider such as Google AdSense or Google AdMob. If and when ads go live:

  • The ad provider may use cookies or advertising identifiers under its own privacy policy (for example Google's). This page will name the provider and link to their policy at that time.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional — the full game stays playable without watching any.
  • The game itself still sends no gameplay data to the ad provider.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children. There is nothing to sign up for and no way to submit personal details.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if advertising or a backup service is added), the updated policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: open an issue at github.com/parya5103/Fletch_Game/issues.
