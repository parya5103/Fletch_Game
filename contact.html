  • Technical problems — a bug, a level that seems impossible, save-data trouble, or anything broken on your device or browser.
  • Feedback and suggestions — what you enjoy, what frustrates you, and what you would like to see next.
  • Privacy questions or requests — anything about the Privacy Policy or your data.
  • Advertising questions — enquiries about advertising on Arrow Out!.

How messaging works

This site has no mail server or private message backend. This form prepares a draft for the Arrow Out! GitHub issue tracker. Preparing a draft with JavaScript enabled keeps it on this page until you choose to open GitHub.

  • GitHub issues are public. Do not include an email address, full name, passwords, payment details, notification subscription values or other private information in the subject or message.
  • Opening the prepared link sends its subject and message to GitHub in the URL, before you submit an issue. The URL may remain in browser history and GitHub logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • A GitHub account is required to post. Your GitHub username and the issue text become public when you submit it. Replies are issue comments; this page does not send email.
  • For privacy questions, describe the problem without personal details. This page provides no private channel for sensitive deletion or access requests. Do not post identifiers in a public issue.
