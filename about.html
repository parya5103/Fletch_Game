About Arrow Out!

A free, offline-first tap-away puzzle · 1000 levels · no sign-up · ad-free today

Arrow Out! is a browser-based arrow puzzle game. Every board is a dense maze of winding arrows, and your job is simple to learn and hard to master: tap arrows away in the right order until the board is empty. It runs directly in supported mobile and desktop browsers — no downloads, no sign-up.

Our goal

We want Arrow Out! to be an enjoyable, accessible, mobile-friendly puzzle experience: quick to open, comfortable to play one-handed on a phone, and just as playable on a desktop browser. It is an independent project with a simple deal — free to play, progress stays on your device, no sign-up. The game is continuously improved: the automated CI suite regenerates all 1000 campaign levels and checks three removal orders, plus 366 daily seeds. Recorded browser checks cover selected desktop engines and mobile viewport sizes; they do not establish compatibility with every browser or physical device.

How to play

  • Tap an arrow — it slithers off the board in the direction its head points.
  • An arrow only launches if its path to the edge is clear — any other arrow in the corridor blocks it.
  • A blocked tap costs one of your 3 hearts. Lose them all and a failure dialog lets you retry. Generated boards are checked for a complete solution.
  • Clear every arrow to win. Stars reward clean runs: 3 stars for no mistakes and no hints.
  • Hints: 3 free per level. Undo brings back your last launch. Restart resets the board.

What makes it different

  • Every level is guaranteed solvable — campaign boards are built in reverse, validated during generation, and checked in CI.
  • 1000 handcrafted-feeling levels — boards grow from 12×12 to dense 65×65, with 20 to 600 real arrow paths. Zoom and pan to inspect dense puzzles.
  • Daily Challenge — a fresh puzzle every date; keep your streak alive.
  • Timed bonus objectives — golden bonus pieces on levels ending in 5 pay extra coins if you clear them before the timer.
  • Skins shop — coins come from play and daily login rewards; unlock 5 board themes and 17 arrow cosmetics (colour palettes and six distinct designs), including free defaults, with live previews before you buy.
  • Achievements and statistics — everything tracked privately on your device.

FAQ

  • Is it free? Yes — no real-money purchases. The game is ad-free today; extra hints currently use a labelled five-second demo; real rewarded ads may be added later — the Privacy Policy explains what that would mean.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. After a successful service-worker installation has cached the game files, gameplay works offline in browsers that support it. Opening or posting a GitHub issue requires a connection. Local reminders work without an external service.
  • Do I need an account? No. Progress saves automatically in browser storage when available. Blocking or clearing storage can prevent progress from being restored.
  • How do I install it? On Android/Chrome: tap Install in the game menu. On iPhone/iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • What are daily reminders? Optional notifications (off by default). Open Settings → Notification & reminder settings to choose 1, 1½ or 2 hours of inactivity. In-game reminders need the page open; optional system notifications need explicit permission and a running page. Closed or suspended browsers cannot schedule these reminders. You can turn them off any time. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Where do I report a bug? Use the Contact page, or open an issue at github.com/parya5103/Fletch_Game/issues.
