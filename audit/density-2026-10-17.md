# Dense campaign — 2026-10-17

Base: `b96b5971383c169ed98efe1e07590b85bc6dfb95` (latest main at task start).

## Implementation

- Monotonic design anchors: 20, 28, 50, 130, 300, 500, 600 arrows at levels 1, 10, 50, 200, 500, 800, 1000.
- Campaign boards grow from 12×12 to 65×65. Paths have distinct occupied cells and aligned heads. A new path's head ray is clear of earlier paths; removing in reverse placement order proves solvability. Any legal removal only deletes obstacles, so other valid orders remain safe.
- The old carver constrained every body cell's exit projection and stranded large areas on large boards. The new carver constrains the actual head exit ray, grows its tail backwards, and checks geometry and solvability before publishing a level.
- Grid occupancy remains the O(1) spatial index for touch and O(grid width) lookup for collisions. Camera transforms map touches into exactly one cell, never overlapping expanded arrow hitboxes.
- Dense overview taps zoom to 44px cells; drag, pinch, wheel, +/− and Fit navigate. Hint focuses the clear arrow. Selection occurs on release; navigation/cancellation cannot cause a mistaken launch.
- Rendering culls offscreen pieces and caches stationary pieces on a viewport-sized layer. DPR is capped at two. At 390px, both canvas layers together use about 4.9 MB of RGBA pixel storage, regardless of zoom. Browser/GPU overhead is additional.
- v2 saves, progress, coins, stars and legacy in-progress geometry remain compatible. Restart deliberately regenerates the selected level under the new progression. Daily challenge rules and 40-entry undo persistence remain unchanged. SW cache advances to v32.
