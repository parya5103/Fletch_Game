About Arrow Out!

A free, offline-first tap-away puzzle · 1000 levels · no sign-up · ad-free today

Arrow Out! is a mobile-first logic puzzle. Every board is a dense maze of winding arrows, and your job is simple to learn and hard to master: tap arrows away in the right order until the board is empty.

How to play

  • Tap an arrow — it slithers off the board in the direction its head points.
  • An arrow only launches if its path to the edge is clear — any other arrow in the corridor blocks it.
  • A blocked tap costs one of your 3 hearts. Lose them all and the level restarts (levels are never unsolvable — there is always a way through).
  • Clear every arrow to win. Stars reward clean runs: 3 stars for no mistakes and no hints.
  • Hints: 3 free per level. Undo brings back your last launch. Restart resets the board.

What makes it different

  • Every level is guaranteed solvable — boards are built in reverse and verified by an automated test suite on every release.
  • 1000 handcrafted-feeling levels — boards grow from 9×9 to dense 12×12, with spike levels every 10th and ring layouts every 50th.
  • Daily Challenge — a fresh puzzle every date; keep your streak alive.
  • Timed bonus objectives — golden bonus pieces on levels ending in 5 pay extra coins if you clear them before the timer.
  • Skins shop — coins are earned only by playing; 16 arrow palettes to unlock.
  • Achievements and statistics — everything tracked privately on your device.

FAQ

  • Is it free? Yes — no purchases. The game is ad-free today; optional rewarded ads (a short ad for an extra hint) may be added later — the Privacy Policy explains what that would mean.
  • Does it work offline? Yes. After the first load, Arrow Out! is an installable web app (PWA) that plays fully offline on phones and desktops.
  • Do I need an account? No. Progress saves automatically on your device and restores itself.
  • How do I install it? On Android/Chrome: tap Install in the game menu. On iPhone/iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • What are daily reminders? Optional notifications (off by default). Turn them on in Settings and the game reminds you 4–5 times a day — you can turn them off any time. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Where do I report a bug? Open an issue at github.com/parya5103/Fletch_Game/issues.
