Arrow Out! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026 · Game progress stays on your device. Optional reminders use external push services.

What the game stores

Arrow Out! has no accounts or sign-in. There is no server database for game progress. All your progress — current level, stars, coins, owned arrow styles and board themes, streaks, achievements and settings — is saved in your browser's localStorage, on your own device. It never leaves your device.

  • Normal gameplay needs no external API. Loading or updating the site contacts GitHub Pages; enabling or disabling reminders contacts Supabase and your browser's push service.
  • The game itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. Progress lives in localStorage, and the service worker keeps cached copies of the game files for offline play — both stay on your device (see Advertising below for what could change if ads are ever enabled).
  • Installing the game (PWA) copies it to your device for offline play; it does not add any new data collection.
  • The Contact page sends nothing to this site: it prepares a GitHub issue in your own browser, and anything you submit there is handled by GitHub under GitHub’s privacy policy.

Daily reminders (optional notifications)

New saves default to reminders off; existing preferences are preserved. Permission is requested only when you enable reminders in Settings. Your browser creates a Web Push subscription containing an endpoint and encryption keys. These are device-linked identifiers, stored in a Supabase database to deliver game reminders. No name, email or game progress is included. The sender runs in GitHub Actions five times a day on a fixed schedule (10:00, 12:30, 17:00, 19:30 and 23:00 India time), regardless of your time zone; delivery may be delayed.

  • Turning reminders off attempts to delete the send-list record and unsubscribe this browser. A connection or permission failure can prevent removal; the game reports failures. Expired endpoints are removed when the sender receives an expiration response. Deletion from the send list does not promise immediate erasure from provider backups or logs.
  • You can revoke notification permission for the site any time in your browser settings. This stops permission to notify; it does not directly delete the Supabase record.

GitHub hosts the site and runs the sender; Supabase stores the subscription; your browser vendor's push service delivers messages. These services receive network metadata such as IP addresses and apply their own retention policies. See GitHub's privacy statement, Supabase's privacy policy, and your browser vendor's privacy policy. Subscription values should never be posted in public issues.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings → Reset all progress resets game progress and preferences. It does not clear the offline cache or unsubscribe notifications; turn reminders off first.
  • In your browser: clearing site data removes local game data. Use notification settings to revoke permission, and turn reminders off in the game before clearing data to request send-list deletion. Clearing local data cannot itself delete the server record.

Advertising

Arrow Out! shows no ads today. Google AdSense has not been integrated: there is no ad SDK, no ad code from any provider, and no ad revenue. The current rewarded-hint screen is a labelled five-second demo. A future release may add advertising (for example, optional rewarded ads — watch a short ad for one extra hint) through a provider such as Google AdSense. If and when advertising is enabled:

  • Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads based on your visits to this site and other websites.
  • Ads may be personalized (based on interests or past visits) or non-personalized. Where the law requires consent for personalized advertising, this site will ask for it first, and personalized ads will stay off until you agree.
  • You can review and change your Google advertising choices at adssettings.google.com, opt out of personalised advertising through industry tools such as aboutads.info/choices, and control or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Ads will never be placed inside gameplay controls, and nothing on this site will ever click, auto-click or incentivise clicking ads.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional — the full game stays playable without watching any.
  • The provider will be named here and linked to their own privacy policy on the day real ads are enabled, along with any data they then need.

Security

Your game progress stays on your device, which limits exposure, and connections use HTTPS. Still, no online service can guarantee absolute security: information that travels over the internet (notification subscriptions, requests to GitHub Pages or Supabase) can in principle be intercepted or accessed by the parties operating those services. Please keep sensitive personal details out of public GitHub issues.

Where you live

Arrow Out! is a small, independent project and this page is a plain-language description of real behaviour, not a legal compliance certification. If you are in a jurisdiction with specific data-protection laws (for example the GDPR in Europe, or India’s DPDP Act), the practices above still apply exactly as described: minimal local storage, one optional notification subscription, and no sale of data. Where the law requires more — consent prompts, or access and deletion rights — it will be added before any feature that needs it. Privacy questions or requests are welcome any time via the Contact page.

Children

Arrow Out! has no accounts, and the game itself never asks for a name or email address; the optional Contact page form only includes what a visitor chooses to type. Optional reminders still process device-linked identifiers and network metadata as described above. Any future advertising integration needs a separate review of age, consent and applicable privacy requirements before launch.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if advertising or a backup service is added), the updated policy will be published on this page with a new last-updated date.

Contact
