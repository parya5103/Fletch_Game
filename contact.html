What you can contact us about

  • Technical problems — a bug, a level that seems impossible, save-data trouble, or anything broken on your device or browser.
  • Feedback and suggestions — what you enjoy, what frustrates you, and what you would like to see next.
  • Privacy questions or requests — anything about the Privacy Policy or your data.
  • Advertising questions — enquiries about advertising on Arrow Out!.

How messaging works

This site has no mail server or private message backend. The form below prepares an email draft for officialgogocompany@gmail.com in your own email app. With JavaScript enabled, the draft stays on this page until you choose to open it.

  • Email is not public, but it is also not encrypted end-to-end. Do not include passwords, payment details, notification subscription values or other high-risk private information in the subject or message.
  • Opening the prepared link opens your email app with the subject and message pre-filled. Nothing is sent until you press send in your email app. You can review and edit the draft before sending.
  • No account on any website is required. Your message reaches the developer's mailbox directly; there is no public comment thread.
  • For privacy questions, describe the problem without unnecessary personal details. Only include what is needed to help you.
